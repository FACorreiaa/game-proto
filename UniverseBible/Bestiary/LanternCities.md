# Lantern Cities bestiary: survival at any cost

Names, classes and broad behaviour are **ART**. Everything deeper is **PROPOSED**.

| Enemy | Class | From the art | In the 2D game (gameplay, not canon) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ratborn | Common | Desperate, quick, unpredictable melee fighter | Darts in, bites, backs off; sometimes breaks off its approach |
| Lantern Gunner | Common | Ranged firepower and area denial | Keeps its distance and throws lanterns that leave the ground burning |
| Smuggler Brute | Elite | High health, heavy area damage | Can't be stopped mid-swing; its slam hits both sides |
| Night Weaver | Elite | Illusions, traps, confusing effects | Sends two images of itself (one hit breaks each; they fade) and lays snares that slow |

The game shows the Night Weaver's images without saying whether they are magic or trickery. That stays open.

## Boss: The Cinder King (ART)

Deals in light, people and time. A multi-phase fight with summoned enemies and environmental hazards. His role
as a broker of desperation fits the themes; the limits of his power are **UNKNOWN**.

- **Game only (gameplay, not canon):** three phases named the Offer, the Debt and the Price. Cane and thrown lanterns;
  then he calls Ratborn, who shield him while they live, and fire bursts from the floor; then a sweep on both sides and
  wider eruptions.
- **Draft line (DRAFT-006):** "Everything has a price. Even light. Especially light." 

**Open:** is the Night Weaver's power magic or trickery? (see `../Systems/Magic.md`)
