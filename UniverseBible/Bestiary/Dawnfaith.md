# Dawnfaith bestiary: fanatics in the name of light

Names, classes and broad behaviour are **ART**. Everything deeper is **PROPOSED**.

| Enemy | Class | From the art | In the 2D game (gameplay, not canon) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Zealot | Common | Aggressive, devoted warrior | Rushes in with a three-cut flurry; presses harder when hurt |
| Radiant Acolyte | Common | Healing and light magic; empowers allies | Stays back; mends hurt allies, otherwise calls light down on a hero |
| Solar Guard | Elite | Heavy armour; protects allies and counters attacks | Shield held forward; a blow on the shield brings an instant bash |

## Boss: The Last Oracle (ART)

Light and dark phases, battlefield control, mind effects. A prophetic figure whose certainty and desire for an
ending drive the fight.

**Do not merge her with Seraphine** ("The Oracle") without an explicit plot decision (Open Question 9).

- **Game only (gameplay, not canon):** three phases named the Dawn, the Dusk and the Ending. Dawn: a staff, a flare
  that dazes anyone looking at her, columns of light, and a "prophecy" strike laid where the hero is heading. Dusk:
  the room darkens, her eclipse dazes anyone looking *away*, she calls Zealots and raises a dark fire from the floor.
  The Ending: flare and eclipse alternate, faster. Whether her sight of the future is real stays UNKNOWN.
- **Draft lines (DRAFT-008):** "I have seen how this ends. The light goes out, and everyone is finally still." /
  "I will not stop it. I will only make it gentle." 

**Note for writers:** these are believers, not monsters. A Zealot has a family; a Solar Guard took an oath.
