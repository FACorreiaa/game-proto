## Boss: The Last Oracle (ART)

Light and dark phases, battlefield control, mind effects. A prophetic figure whose certainty and desire for an
ending drive the fight.

**Do not merge her with Seraphine** ("The Oracle") without an explicit plot decision (Open Question 9).

**Note for writers:** these are believers, not monsters. A Zealot has a family; a Solar Guard took an oath.
